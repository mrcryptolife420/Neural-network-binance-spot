# AI Ops Guidance Policy

Actions are classified as allowed, confirm required, forbidden, or review required.

Allowed examples include diagnostics, check-all, support bundle creation, support bundle verification, runbook opening, report export, evidence inspection, dashboard smoke, redaction self-test, metrics ingest, and governance reminder review.

Forbidden examples include live trading, real orders, account queries, risk bypass, secret reveal, remote upload, and arbitrary shell.
