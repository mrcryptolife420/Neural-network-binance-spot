# A/B Paper Experiments

Roadmap 082 A/B experiments compare champion and challenger policies using local paper observations only. Supported experiment types are champion-challenger, parameter sweep, symbol basket, time slice, and canary.

Metrics include PnL, net PnL, drawdown, risk-adjusted return, trade count, blocked rate, conflict rate, data-quality warnings, liquidity penalty, turnover, rotation churn, watchdog alerts, and policy violations.

Experiments are reproducible through a deterministic seed. Reports include guardrails proving paper-only execution, no account endpoint usage, and no signed endpoint usage.
