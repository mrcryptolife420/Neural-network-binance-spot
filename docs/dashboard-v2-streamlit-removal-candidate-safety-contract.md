# Streamlit Removal Candidate Safety Contract

Removal candidate work is local-only and does not delete Streamlit unless a separate exact-confirm gate passes.

- Dashboard V2 remains limited to `demo`, `paper` and `testnet-readiness`.
- No live mode, signed real-order endpoint, real account workflow or remote telemetry is introduced.
- Streamlit removal is blocked without no-live proof, V2-only smoke, support/evidence proof and rollback archive.
- If the gate fails, Streamlit remains legacy/fallback.
- Reports and archives must be redacted and auditable.
